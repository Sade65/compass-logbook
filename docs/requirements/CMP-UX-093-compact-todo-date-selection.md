# CMP-UX-093 — Compact Todo Date Selection

**Type:** UX
**Priority:** P1
**Status:** Verification
**Target:** v0.2.x

## Requirement

Keep Todo creation focused on intention: Today, Tomorrow, or a chosen future date. Avoid permanently displaying a large calendar.

## Acceptance criteria

- Today is the default.
- Tomorrow is one click.
- Pick date reveals the calendar only when needed.
- The custom Todo calendar does not accept past dates.
- Past work is handled by CMP-FEAT-047 instead of overloading Todo scheduling semantics.
