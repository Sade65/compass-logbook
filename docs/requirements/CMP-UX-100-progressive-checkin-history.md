# CMP-UX-100 — Progressive Check-In History

## Goal
Make historical check-ins compact, aligned, and progressively browsable.

## Requirements
- Do not show redundant dataframe indices or artificial record-number columns; date is the human identifier in this view.
- Show one historical table below the selected-day record.
- Default to five previous check-ins.
- `Show 5 older check-ins` reveals the next batch without replacing the current rows.
- A future iteration may add month filtering / month view.
